# Privacy Policy: Jago Code

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026
**Developer:** [DEVELOPER NAME]
**Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL]

## Summary

Jago Code does not collect, transmit to any server, share, or sell your personal data. The app requests no permissions and has no internet access.

## Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from this app. The app contains no ads, trackers, analytics, third-party libraries, or crash-reporting services of its own.

## Data stored on your device

To work properly, the following is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- **Work session:** the list of open tabs, cursor positions, bookmarks and code folds.
- **Drafts:** temporary copies of unsaved text so it is not lost when the app closes.
- **Settings:** theme, font size and other display preferences.

This data is never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Your files

The app opens and saves files only through Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework), and only files you choose yourself. It does not scan your storage or read any other files. File contents stay on your device.

## Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions, including internet, broad storage, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Purchases through Google Play

This is a paid app. Purchase, payment and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play and are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer receives only aggregated sales reports from Google, without your payment details. Google may also collect technical data (for example Android vitals crash reports) from devices that allow it; this is outside the app's control.

## Children

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children. Because the app collects no data, the developer holds no children's data.

## Security

Because data does not leave your device, its security depends on your device's protections (screen lock, encryption, system updates). Files you save to shared storage or cloud providers via the file picker are subject to those providers' policies.

## Your rights

Because the developer stores no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. You can remove all local data at any time through Android settings (App info → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding internet access), this policy will be updated before the change is released and the effective date will be revised. The latest version is always available at the address where this document is published.
